Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Digital Adventure operates in connection with its use of the official Pinterest API.

1. Application Purpose

Digital Adventure is a privately operated publishing tool developed solely for internal business use. The application is used exclusively by its owner to upload product images and publish them as Pins on an authorized Pinterest Business account.

Each published Pin includes a direct product URL directing users to the associated online store. The application is not made available to the public and does not provide user registration, login, or third-party access.

2. Scope of Operation

The application operates strictly within the permissions granted by the authorized Pinterest Business account. It does not interact with, access, or retrieve data from other Pinterest users.

3. Data Collection and Processing

Digital Adventure does not collect, store, process, analyze, or monetize personal data.

No personal identifiers, email addresses, account credentials, analytics data, or behavioral tracking information are collected from Pinterest users or website visitors.

4. Pinterest API Usage

The Pinterest API is used solely for the following operational purposes:

  • Uploading product images provided by the business owner
  • Attaching product destination URLs to Pins
  • Publishing Pins to authorized Pinterest boards

The application does not request or access additional Pinterest user data beyond what is required for authorized content publishing.

5. Data Storage

The application does not store personal data. Authentication tokens required for API access are stored locally in a controlled environment and are used exclusively for account authorization purposes. These credentials are not shared, transferred, or exposed to third parties.

6. Third-Party Disclosure

Digital Adventure does not sell, distribute, or disclose any data to third parties. The only external service involved in the operation of this application is Pinterest, through its official API infrastructure.

7. Security

Appropriate technical safeguards are implemented to protect API credentials and prevent unauthorized access. The application communicates with Pinterest using secure HTTPS connections.
